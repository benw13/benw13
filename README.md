### NutriStack: iOS app, live on the App Store

<a href="https://github.com/benw13/nutristack-showcase"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/benw13/nutristack-showcase/main/screenshots/today.jpg" width="150" align="right" alt="NutriStack Today screen"></a>

A supplement tracker that checks your whole stack for dangerous combinations, schedules when to take each item, and grades products from a barcode or label scan. I designed, built and shipped it on my own.

- 2,544 interactions mapped across 281 supplements and 298 prescription drugs
- 642 unit tests on ~119K lines of Swift
- Powers [nutristackapp.com](https://nutristackapp.com), a research site with 1,142 indexed pages

[Showcase](https://github.com/benw13/nutristack-showcase) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761284957)

### CourseTwin: AI study workspace, private beta

An AI study tool that won't answer without a source. It turns a student's own lectures and notes into timestamp-cited notes, checked practice questions and an exam study plan.

- 1,105 unit tests plus Playwright browser tests, ~60K lines of TypeScript
- Electron desktop app and React web app on an Express API, deployed on Railway

[Showcase](https://github.com/benw13/coursetwin-showcase)

### Prediction market arbitrage research

Can a $10K account profit from price gaps between Kalshi and Polymarket? I built a system to test it against real fees, latency and order-book depth. No strategy proved profitable, and I stopped each one at a failure signal set in advance.

- 2.07M order-book snapshots, 9,364 matched market groups, ~29K lines of Python

[Code](https://github.com/benw13/prediction-market-research)
